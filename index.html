Delete Your Swapify AI Account

You can permanently delete your Swapify AI account directly from inside the application.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Swapify AI.
  2. Go to My Account.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your request.

Once confirmed, your account and all associated personal data will be permanently deleted from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.
