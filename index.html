How to Request Account Deletion

If you have created an account in Swap Studio AI and wish to delete it, you can submit a deletion request by contacting us through the email address below.

Please include the following information:

  • Subject: Swap Studio AI Account Deletion Request
  • Your registered email address.
  • Your account ID, if available.

Request Account Deletion by Email

What Happens After Your Request?

We will verify your request and process the deletion of your account and associated personal data within 30 days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon completion of your request, we will delete your app account and associated personal data stored by Emadtech, including account information and personal data linked to your account, unless retention is legally required.

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance purposes. Any retained information will be handled according to applicable legal requirements.

Subscriptions and Payments

Deleting your app account does not automatically cancel subscriptions managed through Google Play. Please manage or cancel your subscription separately through Google Play.
